# Supplementary files

Files of the paper's Supplementary Information that are not part of its PDF.

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `Supplementary_Data_1.csv` | Supplementary Data 1: every paired comparison of Supplementary Tables 2, 4 and 8 with its per-seed values (118 rows). Columns: the table, the comparison (first minus second), the measure, the two sides, the conditions and intruder counts pooled, the episode pairs per seed, the estimate (a fraction), the 95% interval and its method, and the value of each seed 4001 to 4030. Written by `python3 -m analysis.make_si_data`; this copy is byte-identical to the file the paper supplies. |
| `Supplementary_Video_1.mp4`, `_2.mp4`, `_3.mp4` | Supplementary Videos 1 to 3: one example episode of the commander, consensus and independent team (main study, ideal network, four intruders, seed 4003), four times real time. Drawn by the replay viewer; `make_videos.py` redraws them from the bundles of `python3 -m analysis.episode_bundles`. `videos.json` lists their episodes and lengths. |

The Supplementary tables' numbers, the Supplementary figures and the other macros are regenerated by `analysis/` and `figures/`
(`analysis/README.md`).
